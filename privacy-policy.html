贝翻译（Bay Translate）隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 23 日

English version below

本政策说明贝翻译浏览器扩展（以下简称“贝翻译”）如何处理你的数据。贝翻译只有一个用途：把你正在浏览的外语网页翻译成你选择的语言，译文显示在原文所在的位置。

我们处理哪些数据

  • 网页内容。贝翻译会读取你正在浏览的网页上的文字，在你的设备上识别语言并翻译，再把译文显示在原文位置。网页文字和译文只在你的设备上处理，不会发送给我们或任何第三方。
  • 设置。自动翻译开关、语言选择、你为各网站设置的翻译规则（包含这些网站的域名）、术语表、外观和界面语言，以及语言包的下载进度。这些设置只保存在你的浏览器本地，不会同步或上传。
  • 翻译缓存。为避免重复翻译，译文会暂存在内存中，浏览器关闭或扩展后台停止后即清除。

网络与语言包

除了下载语言包，贝翻译不联网。第一次使用某种语言时，需要下载这种语言的语言包。安装后，贝翻译还会在后台下载常用语言的语言包（约 0.6–1.6 GB，因浏览器而异），你可以在“外观与设置”中暂停，或关闭“自动准备常用语言包”。

语言包由你使用的浏览器下载，受浏览器厂商（Google 或 Microsoft）的隐私政策约束。下载请求不包含网页内容、译文或浏览记录。

我们不做的事

  • 不收集个人身份信息，不需要注册账号。
  • 不出售用户数据，也不向任何第三方转让用户数据。
  • 不把数据用于翻译以外的目的，不用于广告、数据分析、信用评估或贷款。

贝翻译对用户数据的使用遵守 Chrome 应用商店用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”要求。

数据的删除

你可以随时在术语表中删除术语，或在设置中修改各项选择。卸载贝翻译后，保存在浏览器里的设置和术语表会一并删除。

政策变更

本政策如有变更，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。

Bay Translate Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 October 2026

This policy explains how the Bay Translate browser extension handles your data. Bay Translate has a single purpose: translating the foreign-language web page you are viewing into the language you choose, showing the translation where the original text was.

What we process

  • Website content. Bay Translate reads the text of the page you are viewing, detects its language and translates it on your device, then shows the translation in place. Page text and translations are processed only on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Settings. The auto-translate switch, your languages, the rules you set for websites (including those sites' domain names), the glossary, appearance and interface language, and language pack download progress. They are kept locally in your browser and are never synced or uploaded.
  • Translation cache. To avoid translating the same text twice, translations are kept in memory and cleared when the browser closes or the extension's background stops.

Network and language packs

Apart from downloading language packs, Bay Translate does not go online. The first time a language is used, its language pack is downloaded. After installation, Bay Translate also downloads the packs for common languages in the background (about 0.6–1.6 GB depending on the browser); you can pause this or turn off “Prepare common language packs” under Appearance & settings.

Language packs are downloaded by your browser, under the browser vendor's (Google's or Microsoft's) privacy policy. Download requests never include page content, translations or browsing history.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information, and no account is needed.
  • We do not sell user data or transfer it to any third party.
  • We do not use data for anything other than translation: no advertising, analytics, creditworthiness or lending.

Bay Translate's use of user data complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can delete glossary terms or change any setting at any time. Uninstalling Bay Translate removes the settings and glossary stored in your browser.
